# Platform research: Wix

## Status
Partner-feasibility workspace. Wix supports hosted redirect payment flows, but a provider available to Wix merchants requires PSP onboarding with Wix business development. A WiPay directory button alone does not confirm a supported package.

## Possible routes
- Platform-wide PSP: WiPay/Wix onboarding and partnership required.
- Site-specific Velo integration: possibly useful to one merchant, but not equivalent to a generally installable marketplace provider.

## Next decisions
- Ask WiPay whether Wix PSP approval or an existing provider package exists.
- Confirm territories, methods, support ownership, incident response, and settlement reconciliation.
